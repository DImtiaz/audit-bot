SiteAuditBot

If you found this page in your server logs: hello. Here is who I am and what my bot did.

SiteAuditBot is a website health checker run by one person, Dewan Imtiaz, a freelance web developer. I use it to review the speed, SEO, security settings, accessibility and email setup of websites, and sometimes I send the owner a short, free report with suggestions.

It identifies itself with this User-Agent:

SiteAuditBot/1.0 (+https://dimtiaz.github.io/audit-bot/)

What it does

  • Loads a few public pages, like a normal visitor or search engine would, and reads the response headers and HTML.
  • Reads public information: the TLS certificate, DNS records (for example SPF and DMARC), robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
  • Asks Google's PageSpeed Insights for a speed test of the page.

What it never does

  • No logins, password guessing, form submissions or attack payloads.
  • No attempts to get past firewalls, CAPTCHAs or bot protection. If it is blocked, it stops and reports "blocked".
  • No probing for private files unless the site owner has asked for a full audit in writing.
  • It does not store passwords, keys or other secrets, even if a site exposes one by mistake. If I notice something like that, I tell the owner privately.

How gentle it is

  • At most 2 requests at a time to your site, with at least half a second between requests.
  • A normal check is a handful of requests, not a crawl.
  • If your server answers "too many requests" (429) or "unavailable" (503), it stops for that site.
  • It always follows robots.txt when crawling more than one page.

Opt out

Email me at dewan.imtiaz01@gmail.com with your domain and I will add it to a block list. The bot will not scan it again.

You can also block it in robots.txt. This works for multi-page crawls; for a full block, please email me too:

User-agent: SiteAuditBot
Disallow: /

Your data

  • Results are kept privately on my own machine and are not published.
  • A report is shared only with the site owner, through a private link or PDF.
  • Ask me and I will delete everything I hold about your site: results, screenshots and reports.
